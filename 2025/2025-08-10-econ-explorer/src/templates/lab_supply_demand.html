Teaching Prompts
  1. Baseline: with current parameters, what are p* and q*? Does increasing b_s raise p* or lower it?
    Hint: think slopes vs intercepts. Use the form to tweak b_s.
  2. Incidence: for a per-unit tax τ, who bears more of the burden here? Why?
    Hint: compare ΔCS and ΔPS and the gap p_b - p_s.
  3. Elasticity: if you make demand flatter (increase b_d), what happens to DWL for the same τ?
    Hint: lost trades triangle depends on quantity change.
  4. Quota: set q_cap < q*. Where do p_b and p_s land? What is the quota rent?
    Hint: use inverse demand/supply at q_cap.
  5. Controls: with a price floor p̄ just above p*, how does traded quantity compare with a per-unit tax of similar wedge size?
