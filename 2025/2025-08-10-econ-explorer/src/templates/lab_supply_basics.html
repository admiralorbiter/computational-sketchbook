Great! Now you can see how the market curves respond to your changes. The point where supply meets demand is called the equilibrium.

📚 Economic Terms Glossary
Market Parameters
a_d (Demand Intercept)
Quantity demanded when price is zero
b_d (Demand Slope)
How quickly quantity falls as price rises
a_s (Supply Intercept)
Cost structure parameter for supply
b_s (Supply Slope)
How quickly quantity rises as price rises
Market Outcomes
p* (Equilibrium Price)
Market-clearing price where supply meets demand
q* (Equilibrium Quantity)
Market-clearing quantity traded
CS (Consumer Surplus)
Net benefit to consumers above the price paid
PS (Producer Surplus)
Net benefit to producers above their costs
