Chapter 3: Welfare Analysis

Great! Now you can see the market equilibrium clearly. But what does this mean for society? Let's explore consumer surplus, producer surplus, and total welfare.

The area above the price line and below the demand curve represents consumer surplus - the extra value consumers get. The area below the price line and above the supply curve represents producer surplus.
