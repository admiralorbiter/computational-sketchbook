Chapter 2: First-Price Sealed Bid

Our first auction format is the First-Price Sealed Bid. Here's how it works:

  • All bidders submit their bids simultaneously
  • Highest bidder wins and pays their bid amount
  • No one knows what others bid until it's over

This creates a fascinating strategic dilemma: bid too high and you might overpay, bid too low and you might lose to someone who values the item less than you do!

Chapter 3: Second-Price & Revenue Equivalence

Now let's explore the Second-Price Sealed Bid (also called Vickrey auction):

  • Highest bidder wins but pays the second-highest bid
  • This creates a remarkable incentive: bid your true value!
  • No strategic underbidding needed

Here's the magic: despite different rules, both auction types often generate the same revenue on average. This is the famous Revenue Equivalence Theorem!

Chapter 4: English & Dutch Auctions

Let's explore two more auction formats:

  • English Auction: Open ascending bids, everyone sees the action
  • Dutch Auction: Descending price clock, first to stop wins

These formats reveal information during the auction, creating different strategic dynamics. The English auction can drive prices higher through competitive momentum, while the Dutch auction creates pressure to act quickly!
