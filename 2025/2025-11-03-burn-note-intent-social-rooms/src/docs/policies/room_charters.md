# Room Charters — Principles & Presentation

## Principles
- Be kind; no harassment or hate speech
- No professional advice in health/legal rooms; share resources
- Respect anonymity; no attempts to de-anonymize

## UX Presentation
- Show charter on first post attempt
- Short, scannable bullets; link to full policy

## Enforcement
- Mods can tombstone, slow-mode, shadow-limit, or ban session keys
