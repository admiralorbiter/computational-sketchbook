## 1. Overview
Subject-stratified 11-state Markov chain simulation modeling teacher recruitment, certification, retention, and attrition using IPEDS and Title II federal datasets.

---

## 2. Archaeological Significance
This experiment was originally developed inside the omnibus `protypes` incubator repository during the Summer–Fall 2025 burst. It represents an autonomous single-afternoon probe into domain mechanics, computational modeling, or interactive learning tools.

## 3. Academic Foundations & Institutional Lineage

- **Markovian Mathematical Formulation:** Derived from **Islam & Majumder (2024)** (*Understanding Teacher Attrition in Nebraska Public Schools Through Markov Chain Analysis and Graph Theory*) and the structural labor economics models of **Stinebrickner (2001)** (*A Dynamic Model of Teacher Labor Supply*).
- **Federal Data Integration:** Uses federal Title II and IPEDS state completion figures (U.S. Department of Education, 2023).
- **Institutional Research Synthesis:** Detailed policy benchmarks, stock-flow definitions, and PREP-KC operational applications are cataloged in `PREP-KC/modeling` at [`docs/modeling/teacher-pipeline-research-foundation.md`](https://github.com/PREP-KC/modeling).
