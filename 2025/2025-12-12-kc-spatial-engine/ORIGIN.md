# Origin & Provenance: Kansas City Spatial Engine & MapLibre Vector Substrate (`map`)

- **Original Repository:** `https://github.com/admiralorbiter/map`
- **Development Window:** December 12–23, 2025 (1 intense build day + 1 cleanup return, ~8 commits)
- **Original HEAD SHA:** `eac9abf`
- **Lineage Connection:** The technical infrastructure fork of `map-data`. Proved that local OpenStreetMap (OSM) vector tiles and MapLibre GL could serve as a scalable, high-performance visual substrate onto which external municipal and Census data (ACS/TIGER ZIP choropleths) could be dynamically layered and queried. Descendant domain capabilities live on in `kc-industries`.
- **Consolidation Status:** Full Node/Express vector tile server, SpatialService feature query engine, MapLibre 3D client, Python Census ETL pipelines, and documentation consolidated into `computational-sketchbook/2025/2025-12-12-kc-spatial-engine/`.
