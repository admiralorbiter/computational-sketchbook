# Museum of Pictures — Procedural Infinite 3D Art Gallery

> **Category:** `[SKETCHBOOK EXPERIMENT]`  
> **Date:** March 2, 2025 (~1 hour build)  
> **Stack:** HTML5, JavaScript, Three.js  

## 1. What Was the Idea?
What if an art museum does not have a fixed floor plan, but procedurally generates itself around the visitor as they walk?

## 2. What Was Built?
A modular Three.js environment where `MuseumLayout` dynamically tracks a 2D spatial grid, instantiating rooms, connecting hallways, lighting, and art frames based on player proximity while unloading distant rooms. Rooms are themed across three distinct architectural styles:
- **Classical:** Renaissance & historical portraiture.
- **Futuristic:** Modern, technological, and abstract geometric art.
- **Abstract:** Surreal and contemporary experimental layouts.

## 3. Why It Stopped & Archaeological Value:
A 1-hour exploratory spike proving dynamic 3D chunking and spatial procedural generation in Three.js. Art assets used placeholder/hardcoded image URLs. Preserved as a pure procedural generation experiment.
