How to Interpret These Values:

Max Safe Price

  • For BUY NO bets: The highest price you should pay for a "NO" contract
  • For BUY YES bets: The lowest price you should pay for a "YES" contract
  • If current market price is above this, the bet is overpriced
  • Based on time-sensitive probability analysis and historical volatility

Recommended Limit Price

  • The specific price to set your limit order at
  • Usually 90% of the max safe price for better entry
  • Gives you a buffer to get filled at a better price
  • More realistic than trying to get the absolute best price

Example for Your $116,000 Bet:

  • Current Market: 5¢ (0.05)
  • Max Safe: 0.870 (87¢)
  • Recommended Limit: 0.783 (78.3¢)
  • Interpretation: The current 5¢ is way too low! You could pay up to 87¢ and still have positive expected value. Set a limit order at 78.3¢ for a good entry.

Why 0.97 is "Too High":

  • If the market price is 97¢ (0.97), that's above the max safe price
  • The system calculates this gives negative expected value
  • You should either wait for a better price or skip this bet

Note: The price guidance column should appear in your actual Kalshi table once the Flask app is restarted with the updated code.
