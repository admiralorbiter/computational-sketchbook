How to Use This Data
  • Yes Bid/Ask: Current bid and ask prices for "Yes" contracts (price will be above strike)
  • Mid Price: Average of bid and ask - useful for fair value estimation
  • Volume: Total trading volume for the market
  • Open Interest: Number of outstanding contracts
  • Last Trade: Most recent trade price
  • Daily Markets: These markets predict if BTC/ETH will be above a certain price at 5pm EDT or 11pm EDT

Use these daily market prices in the BTC/ETH Probability Helper to get more accurate probability estimates and Kelly sizing for daily predictions.
