## 1. Concept & Hypothesis

Can players learn and exploit 2-adic / ultrametric distance metrics through gameplay without prior exposure to $p$-adic number theory?

---

## 2. What Was Built

- **Ultrametric World Graph:** Rooms and hallways arranged hierarchically where similarity and longest-shared-prefix bitwise ancestry determine stealth, distance, and guard perception rather than Euclidean coordinates.
- **2-Adic Mechanics Engine:** Player stealth, vision cones, and item unlocks operate via $d_2(x, y) = 2^{-v_2(x - y)}$.
- **Interactive UI & Visualizers:** Tree navigation maps and real-time ultrametric distance indicators.
