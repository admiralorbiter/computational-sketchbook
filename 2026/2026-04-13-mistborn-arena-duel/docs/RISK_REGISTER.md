# Risk Register

## Risk 1 - Overscoping

### Description
Too many mechanics or characters may slow progress and reduce polish.

### Mitigation
Keep MVP to 2 characters and 1 stage.

## Risk 2 - Combat Feels Bad Without Final Art

### Description
Team may misdiagnose visual roughness as gameplay failure.

### Mitigation
Invest in debug readability and placeholder effects early.

## Risk 3 - Physics Instability

### Description
Arcade physics may create edge-case movement issues if overused.

### Mitigation
Keep combat semantics custom and movement constraints explicit.

## Risk 4 - Hard-to-Network Architecture

### Description
Poor separation of gameplay/presentation may block future multiplayer.

### Mitigation
Use input abstraction, state logging, and data-driven moves.
