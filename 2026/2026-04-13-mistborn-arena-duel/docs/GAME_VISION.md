# Game Vision

## Fantasy
Deliver fast, readable duels where players feel clever, mobile, and dangerous.

## Pillars
- readable powers
- movement-driven combat
- small but deep
- local first, online later

## Desired Feel
The game should feel more like a compact tactical duel than a button-mashing brawler.

## Target Match Experience
Within one round, players should:
- understand their movement tools
- use at least one special meaningfully
- experience spacing, defense, and repositioning
- recognize the difference between both characters

## Genre Choice
2D arena duelist with platform elements.

## Why Not a Traditional Fighter First
A traditional frame-precise fighter requires more move complexity, balance work, and animation dependence than this prototype needs.

## Why Not a Physics Party Brawler
The project needs clearer simulation and less chaos to remain Phaser-friendly and multiplayer-expandable later.
