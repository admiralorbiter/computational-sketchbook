# Art Direction

## MVP Visual Strategy
Use placeholder, high-readability visuals first.

## Priorities
1. silhouettes are readable
2. hitboxes and effects are understandable
3. stage interaction points are visible
4. UI is clear

## Placeholder Approach
- colored rectangles or simple sprite silhouettes
- bold direction markers for attacks
- simple VFX for push/pull/lashing

## Style Direction Later
Potential directions:
- clean pixel art
- stylized hand-drawn sprites
- minimalist high-contrast fantasy silhouettes

## Rule
Do not let final art dependency block combat validation.
