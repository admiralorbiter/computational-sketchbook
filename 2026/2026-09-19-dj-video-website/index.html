djon's Blurbs

About me:

Hey! I'm djon (pronounced like Dijon, or DJ-on, or just Jon with a silent D). Inspired by the high-octane hype of Lil Jon, the seamless mashup energy of Girl Talk, and even the occasional classical symphony layer over a modern beat, I bring a unique sound to every event.

Having worked every side of events from setting up and bartending to the DJ booth, my goal is simple: figure out what your crowd loves and keep the dance floor packed all night.

Event Booking & DJ Services:

I love spinning for weddings, private parties, milestone birthdays, anniversaries, corporate parties, and pop-up events. If you need a DJ who actually reads the room, curates the music to fit your guests, and keeps people dancing instead of playing cookie-cutter EDM playlists, let's talk!
