# DJon - Professional DJ Website

A lean, accessible, semantic HTML5 website for **DJon**'s DJ entertainment business based in Kansas City, Kansas.

## Active Pages

- [`index.html`](index.html): Homepage with header, tagline ("When you need a little spice in your life"), photo with his cats, story introduction, core event offerings (weddings, private parties, corporate events), equipment notes, Kansas City service area, and booking CTA.
- [`about.html`](about.html): In-depth background on setting up and bartending weddings, philosophy on curating music for the crowd, musical influences (Lil Jon, Girl Talk, Classical Symphony layers, 2000s bangers, no EDM), sound equipment capabilities, and service area.
- [`contact.html`](contact.html): Comprehensive booking inquiry form with event categories, venue locations, sound needs, and direct inquiry details with placeholder email and phone.

## Status

All semantic HTML structures and copy are populated strictly with user-provided information. No dummy text or assumptions remain. Ready for CSS layout and visual styling.
