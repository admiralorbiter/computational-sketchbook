About DJon

Crowd-first music curation, 2000s bangers, and seasoned event experience.

The Story: Behind the Scenes & In the Booth

From Setting Up & Bartending to the DJ Booth

Before stepping behind the decks, I spent years working weddings and events from every angle—setting up venues, tearing down at the end of the night, and bartending behind the bar. That insider perspective taught me what makes a celebration actually tick: reading the room, timing the energy, and keeping guests engaged and having fun.

My Philosophy: Curating for the Crowd

I believe a great DJ doesn't just push play on a cookie-cutter playlist. The fun challenge of every event is figuring out what music you and your guests love, then building a set that keeps people on the dance floor. Whether it's nostalgic throwback anthems or unexpected mashup moments, it's all about making the night memorable for you.

Musical Style & Influences

Millennial Hits & Early 2000s Bangers
The cornerstone of the party: high-energy 2000s classics, throwbacks, and crowd-pleasing anthems across eras.
Lil Jon Energy & Party Anthems
Unapologetic hype and party energy designed to get hands up and crowds moving.
Girl Talk Mashup Culture
Creative, seamless cross-genre blends and transitions that catch listeners off guard in the best way possible.
Pop, Pop-Punk, Rap & Hip-Hop
Guitar riffs, timeless singalongs, and classic beats rather than generic synthesizer loops. (I stay away from EDM and electronic club sets.)
Classical Layers
As an avid classical fan and Symphony ticket holder, I love weaving melodic classical elements and strings over modern beats for unique sonic textures.

Equipment & Sound Setup

Self-Contained Sound

For small to mid-sized rooms, I bring my own sound system, mixing board, and wireless microphone setup, ensuring clear sound for announcements and punchy sound for music.

Larger Venues & Custom Spaces

For bigger halls, ballrooms, or outdoor festivals, I coordinate directly with venue audio teams or arrange professional rental reinforcement tailored to the space.

Service Area

Based in Kansas City, Kansas. Available throughout the Kansas City metro and open to travel for destination weddings and special events.
