HMC-1 Communications • a year ago • Wednesday, Apr 17 at 6:00 PM • Hickman Mills C-1 Schools
For the Tuesday, April 16, 2024 Board Meeting 
The Hickman Mills C-1 School District is proud to announce the inauguration of its new board members and the
selection of its new board president, marking a signiﬁcant moment in the district's leadership.
 
Bonnaye Mims and Alvin Brooks are now oﬃcial members of the Hickman Mills C-1 School Board after being
sworn in during a Tuesday, April 16 ceremony. Mims, appointed to a three-year term, brings a wealth of experience
and dedication to the board, having served in various capacities within the education and public service sectors.
With a background in the Missouri Department of Mental Health Forensic Services and extensive involvement in
local governance, Mims will make a meaningful contribution to the district's continued success.
 
Alvin Brooks, appointed to a one-year term, adds valuable expertise and a solid commitment to improving
education for all students. As a retired educator from the Kansas City Public Schools, former president of the
Kansas City Board of Police Commissioners, and chair of the board of Hope Academy, Brooks' multifaceted
experience uniquely positions him to advocate for the holistic needs of Hickman Mills students.
 
In addition to swearing in new board members, the Hickman Mills C-1 School Board elected Irene Kendrick as
board president and Anne Coleman as board vice president. Kendrick and Coleman served as co-presidents
during the past year. The inauguration and election ceremony marks a new chapter in the Hickman Mills C-1
School District's journey toward a future of innovation, equity, and excellence in education.

The January board meeting began with a welcome and the call to order, with Zoom meeting information
provided. The adoption of the agenda (Policy BDDB-2) was then conﬁrmed through a roll call. Consent items,
including the approval of minutes (Policy BDDG), were discussed and approved.
Pillar A - Our District: 
4.1 – Superintendent Yaw Obeng provided comments (Policy CBG) highlighting HMC-1 success.
National School Board Recognition Month
HMC One Wireless Campaign
Winter Summit
Lego League
Above and Beyond Award Winner – HMMS 7th Grade student Essynce Love
Neil Smith Community Service Award – Ruskin High School Sr. Camille Long
Pillar B - Our Schools:
5.1 - Assistant Superintendent of Academic Services Katie Roe presented on 8th Grade Center Data in
Action (Policy FB). 
Approximately 90 students participated in this new elective class at the 8th Grade Center, led
by Teacher Denise Stafos. 
The class involved interaction with industry professionals; notably, 34 students earned their
NC3 certiﬁcation (MVA) in Data Science.
5.2 - Superintendent Yaw Obeng, Assistant Superintendent of Academic Services Katie Roe, and
Assistant Superintendent of Operations Kevin Robinson provided an update on the HMC-1 Middle
School redesign plan. This comprehensive plan responds to the pressing need for immediate change
in our district's middle school program, focusing on providing improved programming, investing in our
students, strategically growing our district, and committing to building the future we envision.

--- Page 2 ---
Plan Timeline:
January 2022: BOE Meeting - Initial approval for middle school redesign.
August 2022: Opening the 6th Grade Center (Phase 1).
February 2023: BOE Meeting - Approval for Phase 2 (6th/7th Center and 8th Center).
April 2023: Parent/Community Meeting & Surveys.
November/December 2023: Staﬀ Meetings/Middle School Redesign Committee.
Ongoing: Consultation with DESE Supervisors.
Key Highlights of the Plan:
The current Ervin building returns to being a middle school to be named by the BOE.
The newly renovated Burke Building transitions into the new home of Ervin Elementary School.
Current Ervin Elementary students and staﬀ stay together in the new building.
In the 2024-2025 school year, HMC-1 will have TWO Middle School buildings, both hosting 6th
through 8th graders.
DESE removes Comprehensive/Unaccredited status for both Middle Schools.
Ervin Pre-K remains at its current site as a standalone school, to be named by the BOE.
Pillar C - Our Community: 
Director of Communications and Community Engagement Justin Robinson provided an update on the
Comprehensive School Improvement Plan (CSIP), focusing on communications and community
engagement (Policy CH). The report highlighted:
HMC One Square
Around the Mill Magazine
HMC One Equity Calendar
Social Media Growth
Earned Media Growth
SY 2023-2024 Highlights
Additionally, there was an update on the board election (Policy BBB-1) and an opportunity for
audience comments (Policy BDDH-1).
