- **TY2020**: $149,817 (Grant `202007-8891`, Implementation Phase 1 Tranche 1)
- **TY2021**: $150,000 (Grant `202007-8891`, Implementation Phase 1 Tranche 2)

## 3. Statutory Financial Trajectory (DESE ASBR FY19 to FY24)

- **Comprehensive High-Capacity In-House CTE**: Hickman Mills maintains extensive career academies at Ruskin High School, spending **$3,038,693** on internal CTE instruction (Function 1300) in FY24.
- **Area Career Center Tuition (1921)**: $342,333 in FY24.
- **Total Career Connected Spending**: **$3,381,026 in FY24** (representing 74.8% of the entire 3-district microregion's career spending).
- **Local Absorption Multiplier**: In FY24, Hickman Mills invested **$25.04 in statutory career education for every $1.00 of Kauffman grant money**.
- **Assessed Valuation**: Expanded from $400.9M to $720.6M (+79.7%).
- **Ending Fund Reserves**: Surged from $9.6M to **$53.0M (+453.8%)**.
