## 2. Key Analytical Breakthroughs

### 1. Reconciliation of the $420,000 Three-Year Aggregate
Funderscope reported an IRS-derived aggregate of **$420,000 across three funded tax years from 2021 to 2024**. The primary filings completely explain this figure:
- **TY2021**: $150,000 (disbursement of grant `202007-8824`)
- **TY2022**: $0 (gap year)
- **TY2023**: $135,000 (disbursement of grant `202307-14162`)
- **TY2024**: $135,000 (disbursement of grant `202307-14162`)
- **Sum (2021–2024)**: $\$150,000 + \$135,000 + \$135,000 = \mathbf{\$420,000}$ across exactly **three funded tax years**.

### 2. Resolution of the Early Funded Lifecycle (2019–2021)
- **Summer 2019 Design & Plan Phase**: Grant `201904-6368` provided **$70,435** specifically to develop the three-year strategic plan for Real World Learning.
- **Implementation Phase 1**: Grant `202007-8824` committed **$300,000** across two annual tranches of **$150,000** in TY2020 and TY2021.
- Combining all years (2019 through 2024), the Kauffman Foundation paid a total of **$640,435** directly to Consolidated School District No. 4.

### 3. Resolution of the "Right-Hand Edge"
A critical question for this study was: *When did direct philanthropic grant commitments terminate or transition?*
- In the **TY2023 filing**, Kauffman reported $135,000 paid during the year and explicitly scheduled **$135,000 in Part XV, Line 3b ("Approved for Future Payment")** under grant `202307-14162`.
- In the **TY2024 filing** (covering the calendar year ending December 31, 2024), Kauffman reported that final $135,000 as paid.
- Crucially, in that same **TY2024 filing, Part XV, Line 3b contains $0 approved for future payment to Grandview C-4**.
- Therefore, as of **December 31, 2024**, grant `202307-14162` had concluded its active payout schedule, and no new multi-year continuation grant was encumbered on Kauffman's balance sheet.
