## 1. Question

After direct Kauffman Real World Learning (RWL) grant support ended at Grandview C-4 on December 31, 2024, did the underlying practices become institutionally sustained, rebranded/absorbed, or decay?

---

## 2. What Is Now Known

- **Empirical Classification**: **Structural Local Absorption with Corporate Co-Investment**
  - **Zero Evidence of Decay**: District career instruction doubled, partnerships expanded, and leadership maintained continuity through mid-2026.
  - **Fiscal Crowd-In (Not Substitution)**: Statutory Career and Technical Education (Function 1311–1391) expenditures rose by +95.7% from $169,978 (FY19) to $332,616 (FY24). Total career-connected spending ($473,232 in FY24) surpassed the annual Kauffman grant disbursement ($135,000) by **3.51 to 1**.
  - **Tax Base Expansion & Budget Headroom**: Assessed valuation grew +69.3% ($474.7M to $803.8M), local tax revenue grew +64.0% ($34.1M to $55.9M), and total ending fund balances rose +140.8% ($20.0M to $48.2M), providing strong local absorption capacity.
  - **Personnel Anchoring**: 2,283 staff records across 21 Wayback snapshots show zero ephemeral soft-money coordinator titles. Curriculum & Instruction leadership has been continuously helmed by Assistant Superintendent Prissy LeMay since January 2023.
  - **Executive Succession Continuity**: Long-time Superintendent Dr. Kenny Rodrequez (2014–2026) transitioned in July 2026 to Dr. Stephanie Amaya, who served as Assistant Superintendent of HR in Grandview since 2021.
  - **Operational Contracts & Corporate Co-Investment**: Post-direct-grant board actions renewed 200 hours of AWS welding with T&L Welding Academy, added clinical CNA/Phlebotomy training via Between Me 2 You, and secured $125,000 in corporate pathway capital from Honeywell FM&T / KCNSC.

## 3. Tri-District Microregion Comparative Expansion (Grandview, Center, Hickman Mills)

The comparative empirical pipeline has now fully harvested, reconciled, and synthesized all three contiguous South Kansas City school districts across the full 2018–2026 trajectory:

### A. Statutory State Finance & Philanthropic Grant Accounting
- **Synchronized Sunset**: All three districts received nearly identical six-year grants totaling **~$630k–$640k**, and **all three direct grant commitments expired simultaneously on December 31, 2024** ($0 approved for future payment on TY2024 Form 990-PF).
- **Refutation of Crowding-Out**: In **none** of the three districts did Career & Technical Education spending decline. Combined microregional career spending grew from **$3.71M (FY19) to $4.52M (FY24)**.
- **Pervasive Local Absorption Capacity**: Backed by assessed valuation growth (+58.7% to +79.7%), combined district reserves grew from **$44.0M to $126.3M (+187.3%)**.
