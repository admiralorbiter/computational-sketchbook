Incident IQ Tech Ticket & Maintenance Requests

Staff

Want the shared doc of these directions with images?  You can find them here!

1.      Go to https://center58.incidentiq.com  (You might want to make a favorite or desktop shortcut to this location;  we will not force it out.) 
2.      Select Microsoft Azure to log in.
3.      Click the +New Ticket button.  
4.      Select the category of your ticketing need.  Note: here is where you select both tech issues as well as facilities/maintenance requests.
5.    Once you have the category selected, there will be additional issues to select in identifying the purpose of your ticket.  Then complete the following screen for additional, defining information about the issue.  (Scroll down to see descriptions.)
6.       Describe the issue in more detail. 
7.       Use the drop down menu to select the room where the issue is located.
8.       Identify if the ticket is urgent.
9.       Identify if there is any student records or personally identifiable information of either staff or students that needs to be protected.  
            (Selecting YES here will prevent emails from being sent about the progress of the ticket.)
10.     Are there other users to notify?  Identifying someone here will send email alerts when the ticket gets updated.  
           The ticket submitter will already receive the email updates by default.
11.       Are there any additional files or screenshots that will help in identifying the problem?  Upload for the techs to reference.
12.      Finally, submit the ticket.

 

If you have any questions about this please contact any of us in the technology department.

Thanks

Request PD Funds

 

Please complete this form to request funds to attend a workshop or conference using district PD funds. Once you submit the form, send an email to your PDC building rep and Kayla Eppel to let them know you have submitted a request.

You need to submit this form as soon as possible or by the last Friday of the month. PDC will review each request on the first Tuesday of each month during our meeting. If/when your request is approved, you will receive additional information regarding next steps to register/pay.

Your registration will be paid through a P.O. and is NOT a reimbursement to individuals. Please plan ahead with several weeks' notice when submitting this request.

Collective Bargaining

The Collective Bargaining Agreement is a memorandum agreement which reflects the results of collective bargaining negotiations between the Center Education Association (CEA )and Center School District .

 

The purpose of this Agreement is to achieve and maintain harmonious relations between the Center School District Board of Education, Administration and the Center Education Association, to provide the highest quality educational programs for the students of the Center School District, and for equitable and peaceful adjustment of differences which may arise, and to set forth the understanding reached between the parties with respect to various conditions of employment.

This Agreement has been jointly created by representatives of the Board of Education and the Center Education Association through the use of the Modified Interest Based Bargaining (MIBB) process. We are committed to continued use of the MIBB process in our work together, and to resolutions of conflict whenever possible through achievement of consensus upon acknowledgment of all parties interest.

Hive Society - Staff Appreciation Committee

 

 
Hive Society Mission: 

Hive Society is a collective dedicated to enhancing our climate and culture by recognizing  the caring individuals committed to fostering belonging and connection in the Center family. 

Recognition Days:  We will use DESE’s calendar for special staff recognition days.  Hive Society will be recognizing groups/individuals on given days with the following: 

  • CSD Social Media Posts 
  • All-District Email 
  • Special Delivery of thank you cards and snacks and/or trinkets 

Good Buzz/Celebrations: 

  • We will be asking staff to share photos with us of Good Buzz, like :   
    • Have a major life event like a wedding or baby?   
    • Earn a new degree, certification, or credential?  
    • Have a child graduating from HS or college?   
    • Are you volunteering somewhere and want to share the love?  

Let us know so we can spread the buzz.  We can’t spread good buzz unless we know about it, so we are encouraging staff to share freely.  We will ask for permission to share photos, news, etc. if not self-submitted.  To share, email hivesociety@center.k12.mo.us 

Monthly District-Wide Hive Society Awards: 

We are re-vamping our monthly nomination program to align with our Strategic Plan and Core Values.  Every month we will ask all staff to nominate colleagues who demonstrate the monthly initiative.  We’ll collect all nominations, ask for a building level “winner” and then ask our District PREPARE Team to choose one individual for District-wide recognition.  (This will be done blindly with no names/buildings attached, just reason for nomination.) 

  • Please, help us garner nominations from your staff.  A special nomination form and details will be sent to all staff this week.  We’ll surprise all building-level nominees with a special gift and District-wide honorees will be recognized at BOE meetings and treated to a special red-carpet meal with Dr. Cargile.   

Nomination Categories: 

  • September: This person celebrates the things that make us all unique.  This person is intentional about including diverse perspectives and ideas in decision-making.   
  • October: Someone that exemplifies our core value of: High Expectations for Student Success – include creativity etc infographic. 
  • November: Someone that exemplifies our core value of safe and respectful schools 
  • December: Someone that exemplifies our core value of: Lifetime community and family-centric relationships – this person is visible in our community, builds rapport with our families.  This person has Center spirit! 
  • January: Someone that exemplifies our core value of: Highly qualified, diverse staff – this person is a leader among his/her peers, is a lifelong learner and respectful of differences.  Real-world learning opportunities, problem solvers with fresh ideas and approaches.
