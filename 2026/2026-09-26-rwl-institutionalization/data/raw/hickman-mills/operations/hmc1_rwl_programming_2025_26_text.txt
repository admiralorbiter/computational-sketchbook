Health Start Academy- 9th through 12th grade
Prep-KC’s HealthStart academy gives students the skills and experiences they need to 
accelerate their entry into the health sciences professions. HealthStart students get 
hands-on experience in a variety of health science fields including nursing, dentistry, 
medicine, and pharmacy. These hands-on experiences are complemented with college 
coursework and workforce certification opportunities that allow HealthStart students to 
graduate from high school with both workforce credentials such as Certified Nursing 
Assistant, Certified Medical Assistant, Phlebotomy or  Sterile Services credentials as well as 
significant progress towards an associates’ degree. Enrollment in the PLTW Biomed 
program recommended.
Contact: Stephanie.anderson@hickmanmills.org

Social Justice Pathway
Introduction to Social Justice (10/11/12)
 - this 2nd semester (Spring 2022) elective course 
will explore Black History in America. Students will have the opportunity to engage in 
discussions around race and equity. This class will be taught by a Ruskin teacher and is not 
dual credit.
What’s Race Got to Do With It? (11/12)
 - This is a semester long course dual enrollment 
course taught by a UMKC instructor. The class will explore racism in Kansas City. (dual credit 
is paid for by the district)
Criminal Justice (11/12) - This course examines the history of the legal process and the 
administration of justice in the American system. Students analyze the intersectionality 
between race and justice, police functions through adjudication, current events and major 
crimes.

Student Law Academy
What is the Student Law Academy? 
The Student Law Academy’s (SLA) mission is to provide under-served urban 
high school juniors and seniors, who would not otherwise have the opportunity, 
meaningful exposure to careers in the legal 
profession and a broad overview of the legal system.  SLA collaborates with 
local law firms, courts, and other legal entities to provide students with a 
dynamic, paid summer program. SLA aspires to serve as a future pipeline to 
diversify Kansas City's legal community.
 What are the requirements & suggested/not required prerequisites? 
●
2.5 GPA or higher
●
Strong interest in pursuing a career within the legal profession
●
Engaging and inquisitive
