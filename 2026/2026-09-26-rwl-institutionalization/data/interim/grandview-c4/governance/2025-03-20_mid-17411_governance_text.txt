The Board Brief provides information about what took place during our board meetings. It provides details on Board discussions, actions and items of interest to our community. Please check Board Briefs often to stay up to date on the important decisions being made in our district.
Regular Open Meeting Brief – March 20, 2025
The entire meeting agenda can be viewed at:  March 20, 2025 BOE Meeting
Program Evaluations
The Board was presented with program evaluations for Title I Early Childhood and Parents As Teachers (PAT). The goal of the early childhood program is to provide early intervention for educationally at-risk students.  During the 2023-24 school year, the district served 32 pre-kindergarten students with full day programming focused on language and literacy development, early numeracy, and social-emotional development.   PAT served 213 students up to 5 years of age during the 2023-24 school year.  Their goal is to increase kindergarten readiness through early learning experiences.  Over 94% of parents surveyed strongly agree they were satisfied with the PAT program.
Seal of Biliteracy
The Board approved the implementation of the Seal of Biliteracy which is an award given by a school district to recognize high school graduates who have attained proficiency in English and one or more additional languages. The purpose of the Seal is to acknowledge and celebrate the value of multilingualism, encourage the study of world languages, and validate the hard work of students who achieve biliteracy. Earning this distinction provides students with a valuable credential for college admissions, scholarship opportunities, and future employment in a global workforce.
Estimated Tax Levy
Districts are required to annually project estimated, non-binding tax levy rates to be submitted to the County Clerk in April.  The Board approved an estimated tax levy rate of $4.3897 per $100 Assessed Valuation for General and Teacher (Combined) Operating funds, and $1.3666 for the Debit Service fund, for a total estimated levy rate of $5.7563 for 2025.  This reflects a slight increase of $0.0943 over 2024. Official tax levy rates are set annually in the fall after a public tax hearing.
Strategic Plan Review
Administration reviewed progress toward goals in the Focus Areas of Success Ready Students, Workplace Excellence, Operational Readiness and Climate & Culture.   The Missouri School Improvement Program (MSIP) recommends schools districts develop an ongoing, written strategic plan which directs the overall improvement of its educational programs and services. The strategic plan includes goals, objectives and action steps which direct the improvement efforts of the district for a 3-year period.  The Board of Education approved the current District Strategic Plan for 2024-2027 in June 20, 2024.

ITEM [2:5] e. Estimated Tax Levies (ID: mgdm4vobtRUST7EeycOSZQ==)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Background]:
Senate Bill 711, which became effective August 28, 2008, contains a provision whereby the district must project estimated, non-binding, tax levy rates to be submitted to the County Clerk by April 8 of each year.
Effective January 1, 2009, per Section 137.180 RSMo, in a year the assessor increases the valuation of real property, the county clerk on or before March 15 shall send estimated assessed valuation amounts to the school district that is in a county with a charter form of government, other than any county adopting a charter form of government after January 1, 2008, (Jackson, Jefferson, St. Charles, and St. Louis Counties). Each school district shall then calculate and submit an estimated non-binding property tax rate to the county clerk's office by April 8 in odd-numbered years, as required by Section 137.243, RSMo. (ATT 1)
This is a reassessment year within Jackson County and the district recently received preliminary assessed valuation (AV) information.  The preliminary AV within the district increased from $791,686,709 to $883,926,318.
Based on the preliminary assessed valuation provided by Jackson County, the computed maximum Operating tax rate ceiling is $4.3987 per $100 AV.
The Debt Service calculated ceiling is $1.8358; however, Administration is recommending a voluntary reduction by the school district of .4692 and approval of an estimated 2025 tax levy rate of $1.3666 per $100 AV for debt service.

[Recommendation]:
Approve an estimated tax levy rate of $4.3897 per $100 AV for General and Teacher (Combined) Operating Funds, and $1.3666 for the Debt Service Fund, for a total estimated tax levy rate of $5.7563 for 2025.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Background]:
The Seal of Biliteracy is an award given by a school district to recognize high school graduates who have attained proficiency in English and one or more additional languages. The purpose of the Seal is to acknowledge and celebrate the value of multilingualism, encourage the study of world languages, and validate the hard work of students who achieve biliteracy. Earning this distinction provides students with a valuable credential for college admissions, scholarship opportunities, and future employment in a global workforce.
By adopting the Seal of Biliteracy, Grandview C-4 School District demonstrates its commitment to recognizing the linguistic and cultural assets of our students and preparing them for success in an increasingly interconnected world.
The accompanying resolution formally establishes the Seal of Biliteracy within the Grandview C-4 School District and outlines the district’s commitment to supporting students in achieving this recognition.

[Recommendation]:
Approve the implementation of the Seal of Biliteracy for the Grandview C-4 School District, beginning on March 21, 2025 and adopt the accompanying resolution in support of this initiative.
