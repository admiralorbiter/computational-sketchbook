**To:**  
Custodian of Records

**From:**  
Kansas City Metropolitan Education Capacity Study Research Team  
Contact Email: `research@kceducationcapacity.org`  
Date: September 24, 2026  

**Subject: Missouri Sunshine Law Request — Electronic Master Schedule Section Headcount Exports (SY 2024–25, 2019–20, 2014–15)**

Dear Custodian of Records:

Pursuant to the **Missouri Sunshine Law, RSMo Chapter 610**, I am requesting an electronic copy of existing public records maintained within Kansas City Public Schools’ Student Information System (Infinite Campus / Tyler) detailing course section enrollments.

#### 1. Description of Requested Records
We request an electronic data export (CSV, Excel, or delimited format) of the district’s **Fall Master Schedule / Course Section Enrollment Report** (as of the official October fall count date) for the following three school years:
- **SY 2024–2025** (Current Academic Year)
- **SY 2019–2020** (Pre-pandemic Benchmark)
- **SY 2014–2015** (Historical Baseline)

*(If historical archives for 2014–15 or 2019–20 are not readily accessible, please provide the file for SY 2024–2025).*

#### 2. Requested Data Fields
To avoid custom programming, we request standard fields routinely included in an administrative master schedule export:
1. `School Year` (e.g., 2024–25)
2. `School Name` or `Building Code` (e.g., Lincoln College Prep, Central, East, Southeast, Middle Schools, Elementary)
3. `Term / Semester` (e.g., Semester 1 / Fall)
4. `Course Code / Course Number`
5. `Course Title` (e.g., "Algebra I", "Biology", "English 9", "Grade 3 Homeroom")
6. `Department / Subject Area`
7. `Section Identifier / Section Number`
8. `Period / Block`
9. `Teacher Identifier` (Teacher Last Name, Missouri Educator ID, or a consistent de-identified staff ID)
10. `Section Enrollment Count` (Total active student headcount enrolled in that section)
11. *Optional / If readily maintained in the same export:* Count of students with an IEP, 504 Plan, or English Learner classification in that section.

#### 3. Privacy & FERPA Compliance
**No personally identifiable student information (PII) is requested.** This request seeks aggregate section-level counts only (numbers of students per class). Because no individual student names, birthdates, addresses, or student ID numbers are requested, these records contain no FERPA-protected information and require no student record redactions.

#### 4. Fee Waiver & Cost Limitation
Pursuant to **RSMo § 610.026.1(1)**, we request a waiver of fees. This request is submitted for academic, non-commercial public interest research examining educator capacity, staffing allocation, and classroom distributions across the Kansas City metropolitan area. The findings will be made freely available to the public and educational policymakers.

If a fee waiver is not granted, please provide an itemized estimate before incurring any costs. **Please obtain written authorization if estimated processing fees exceed $50.00.**

#### 5. Format & Statutory Response Time
Please provide the records electronically via email attachment or secure file transfer link. As provided by **RSMo § 610.023.3**, please respond to this request within **three (3) business days**.

Thank you for your assistance and dedication to public transparency in education.

Sincerely,  
Kansas City Metropolitan Education Capacity Study Research Team
