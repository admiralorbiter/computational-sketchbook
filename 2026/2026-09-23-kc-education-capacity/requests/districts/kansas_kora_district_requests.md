# Turnkey Kansas Open Records Act (KORA) Request Letters
## Target Districts: USD 512, USD 233, USD 229, USD 500, USD 458

The following letters are fully drafted and formatted for transmission under the **Kansas Open Records Act (KORA), K.S.A. 45-215 through 45-223**.

### Request 1: Shawnee Mission Public Schools (USD 512)

**To:**  
Custodian of Public Records  
Shawnee Mission School District (USD 512)  
Center for Academic Achievement  
8200 W. 71st Street, Overland Park, KS 66204  
Email: `openrecords@smsd.org` / `communications@smsd.org`

**From:**  
Kansas City Metropolitan Education Capacity Study Research Team  
Contact Email: `research@kceducationcapacity.org`  
Date: September 24, 2026  

**Subject: Kansas Open Records Act Request — Electronic Master Schedule Section Enrollment Exports (SY 2024–25, 2019–20, 2014–15)**

Dear Custodian of Records:

Pursuant to the **Kansas Open Records Act (K.S.A. 45-215 et seq.)**, I am requesting an electronic copy of existing public records maintained within the district’s Student Information System (e.g., Skyward / Synergy / Canvas) detailing course section enrollments.

#### 1. Description of Requested Records
We request an electronic data export (CSV, Excel, or delimited text format) of the district’s **Fall Master Schedule / Course Section Enrollment Report** (as of the official fall count date, approximately September 20) for the following three school years:
- **SY 2024–2025** (Current Academic Year)
- **SY 2019–2020** (Pre-pandemic Benchmark)
- **SY 2014–2015** (Historical Baseline)

*(If historical archives for 2014–15 or 2019–20 are not readily accessible, please provide the file for SY 2024–2025).*

#### 2. Requested Data Fields
To avoid custom reporting, we request standard fields typically included in an administrative master schedule export:
1. `School Year` (e.g., 2024–25)
2. `School Name` or `Building ID` (e.g., SM East, SM North, SM Northwest, SM South, SM West, Middle Schools, Elementary)
3. `Term / Semester` (e.g., Semester 1 / Fall)
4. `Course Code / Course Number`
5. `Course Title` (e.g., "Algebra I", "AP Chemistry", "English 9", "Grade 2 Homeroom")
6. `Subject / Department`
7. `Section Number / Identifier`
8. `Class Period / Block`
9. `Teacher Identifier` (Teacher Last Name, State License ID, or a consistent de-identified staff ID)
10. `Section Enrollment Count` (Total active student headcount enrolled in the section)
11. *Optional / If readily maintained in the same export:* Count of students with an IEP, 504 Plan, or English Learner classification in that section.

#### 3. Privacy & FERPA Compliance
**No personally identifiable student information (PII) is requested.** This request seeks aggregate section-level headcounts only (i.e., numbers of students per class). Because no individual student names, birthdates, addresses, or student ID numbers are requested, these records contain no FERPA-protected information and require no individual student record redactions.

#### 4. Fee Waiver & Cost Limitation
Pursuant to **K.S.A. 45-219(e)**, we request a waiver of fees. This request is submitted for academic, non-commercial public interest research examining instructional capacity and classroom staffing across the Kansas City metropolitan area. The findings will be made freely available to the public and educational policymakers.

If a fee waiver is not granted, please provide an itemized estimate before incurring any costs. **Please obtain written authorization if estimated processing fees exceed $50.00.**

#### 5. Format & Statutory Response Time
Please provide the records electronically via email attachment or secure file transfer link. As provided by **K.S.A. 45-218(d)**, please acknowledge receipt of this request within **three (3) business days**.

Thank you for your assistance and dedication to public transparency in education.

Sincerely,  
Kansas City Metropolitan Education Capacity Study Research Team
