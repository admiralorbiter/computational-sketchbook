# Pilot School Conditions Profile Comparison

**Two Metro High Schools with Identical Macro PTR (15.0:1) and Identical Enrollment (1,200 Students)**

| Operational Dimension | School A (Balanced Schedule) | School B (High-Friction Schedule) | Regulatory / Analytical Meaning |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Reported Macro PTR (CCD)** | **15.0:1** | **15.0:1** | *Identical administrative ratio reported to state & public.* |
| **Unweighted Mean Section Size** | 22.6 | 22.6 | Basic section average. |
| **Median Section Size** | 23.0 | 22.0 | What a typical classroom looks like. |
| **Student-Weighted Mean Class Size** | **22.7** | **24.7** | **What the typical student actually experiences.** |
| **Student-Weighted Median Class Size** | 23.0 | 25.0 | 50th percentile student experience. |
| **75th / 90th Percentile Section Size** | 24 / 25 | **29 / 32** | Exposes extreme classroom crowding tail. |
| **% of Students in Classes > 25** | 5.6% | **49.5%** | Above MSIP 6 recommended standard (Grades 7–12). |
| **% of Students in Classes > 30** | 0.0% | **23.6%** | Severe instructional crowding. |
| **% of Students in Classes > 33** | 0.0% | **7.9%** | **Exceeding MSIP 6 maximum allowable standard.** |
| **Compound Complexity Sections (IEP/EL)** | 2.8% | **21.7%** | High headcount ($\ge 25$) + High Need ($\ge 20\%$ IEP/ELL). |
| **Median Teacher Student-Seat Load** | 113 | **116** | Daily student contact seats (*Jenkins* metric). |
| **Novice vs. Veteran Avg. Preps** | 1.7 vs. 1.4 | **3.0 vs. 1.7** | Equity check: Are new teachers loaded with preps? |
| **Novice Compound Class Exposure** | 2.7% | **19.6%** | Share of novice sections with severe compound need. |
| **Teachers Facing Planning Deficit (<250m)** | 0.0% | **0.0%** | Violations of MSIP 6 250 min/wk planning rule. |

### Key Takeaways for Policy & Operational Pilot
1. **The Ratio Illusion:** Both schools employ exactly 1 teacher per 15 students (PTR 15.0:1) and report an identical unweighted section average of 22.6. Yet in School B, **49.5% of students sit in classes exceeding the MSIP 6 recommended cap of 25**, 23.6% sit in classes >30, and **7.9% sit in classes exceeding the state regulatory ceiling of 33**.
2. **The Novice Teacher Tax:** In School B, novice teachers are assigned an average of **3.0 distinct course preparations** (vs. 1.7 for veterans), and **19.6% of their sections carry compound high-need complexity** (large class sizes combined with concentrated IEP/ELL populations), directly driving early-career educator burnout.
3. **Actionable Without New Surveys:** 100% of these metrics were derived strictly from the relational linkage of October Course Assignment, Student Assignment, and Screen 18 compliance records already submitted annually to DESE.
