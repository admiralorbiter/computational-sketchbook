# Track C: Elementary School Yearbook Photography Sampling Protocol
## Independent Non-Administrative Triangulation of Classroom Capacity

---

## 1. Research Justification & Theoretical Objective

Throughout Phases 1 through 3C and Task 004A, all capacity analyses have relied on administrative data submitted by school districts to state agencies (KSDE, MO DESE) or federal authorities (NCES CCD, OCR CRDC). While Task 004A/004A.1 validated that headline pupil/teacher ratios conceal large secondary class sizes via the **Allocation Wedge** (+3.0 to +5.1 students unweighted median; +1.5 to +2.8 class-weighted), administrative collections remain subject to institutional reporting rules, course taxonomy aggregation, and potential reporting artifacts.

**Track C** establishes an **independent, physical, non-administrative measurement protocol** using published school yearbooks.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Sample Characterization:** This protocol constitutes a **stratified purposive validation sample**, not a statistically representative probability sample of the entire 9-county metropolitan area. Its scientific purpose is to test: *Does an entirely non-administrative, physical photographic artifact corroborate the presence and magnitude of the administrative class-size wedge?*

### Why Elementary Homerooms?
1. **The Purest Test of Class Size:** In grades K–5, students spend the vast majority of the instructional day in a single, self-contained homeroom classroom under the care of one certified teacher. There is no departmentalized period rotation, no elective schedule dilution, and no honors/remedial section stratification.
2. **Photographic Ground Truth:** Elementary yearbooks publish annual composite classroom portraits (grids of individual student photos grouped by teacher and grade) or panoramic whole-class photos. Every child assigned to that classroom is photographed or cataloged on the "Not Pictured" roster.
3. **Historical Permanence:** Yearbooks are physical documents printed and distributed to hundreds of families in May of each school year. They are impervious to retroactive administrative adjustments, metric redefinitions, or electronic data system migrations.

## 3. Feasibility Pilot Protocol (2 Schools × 2 Anchor Years)

Before executing physical collection across all 36 school-yearbooks, the research team will execute a **Phase 1 Feasibility Pilot** on two accessible benchmark campuses across two anchor years (2014–15 and 2024–25):
1. **Kansas Pilot School:** Corinth Elementary (Shawnee Mission USD 512).
2. **Missouri Pilot School:** Hale Cook Elementary (Kansas City Public Schools).

### Pilot Feasibility Criteria:
- **Homeroom Structure Stability:** Confirm whether yearbooks in both eras consistently organize student portraits by distinct homeroom teacher and grade level, rather than by alphabetical grade-wide rosters without teacher assignment.
- **Attrition & Not-Pictured Frequency:** Assess the frequency of "Not Pictured" text listings and verify whether group panoramic photos or individual composite grids predominate.
- **Replication Success:** If both pilot schools demonstrate clear homeroom layouts with $< 5\%$ ambiguous portraits, authorization is granted to proceed with full 12-school acquisition.

## 5. Classroom Enumeration & Photometric Protocol

### 5.1 Counting Rules:
1. **Individual Composite Grid Pages:**
   - Enumerate every student portrait thumbnail within the designated homeroom border.
   - **Exclusion:** The teacher portrait (top-left or enlarged) and any designated paraprofessional / student teacher portrait are strictly excluded from the student headcount.
   - **Inclusion of "Not Pictured":** Students explicitly listed in caption text as enrolled but absent on picture day (e.g., *"Not Pictured: Marcus Davis, Chloe Smith"*) are added to the count.
2. **Panoramic / Whole-Class Photo Pages:**
   - Enumerate student faces left-to-right, row-by-row, cross-referencing printed text captions to ensure an exact 1:1 match.
3. **Specialized Homerooms:**
   - Self-contained SPED or intensive resource classrooms are flagged (`is_specialized = 1`) and analyzed separately from general education classrooms.

### 5.2 Ledger Schema (`data/raw/yearbooks/yearbook_homeroom_ledger.csv`):
```text
record_id               # Unique ID: YBK_{NCES_ID}_{YEAR}_{GRADE}_{TEACHER}
nces_school_id          # 12-digit NCES school code
school_name             # School name
school_year             # Academic year (e.g. 2024-25)
grade_level             # K, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
teacher_name            # Last name of classroom teacher
photo_type              # composite_grid | group_photo
portrait_student_count  # Count of photographed student faces
not_pictured_count      # Count of students listed in text caption but not pictured
total_homeroom_students # portrait_student_count + not_pictured_count
is_specialized          # 0 = General Education Homeroom, 1 = Self-contained SPED
coder_initials          # Initials of researcher extracting data
source_citation         # Library archive, call number, or physical book donor
```

## 6. Inter-Rater Reliability (IRR) & Quality Control

To guarantee photometric enumeration integrity:
1. **Dual-Blind Sample:** A random **15% subsample** (~100 classrooms) will be independently enumerated by two separate researchers without access to the other's counts.
2. **Primary Reliability Criteria (Agreement & Absolute Error):**
   - **Exact Agreement Rate:** $\frac{\text{Classes with Identical Counts}}{\text{Total Dual-Coded Classes}} \times 100$ (Pre-set Standard: $\ge 90\%$).
   - **Mean Absolute Difference (MAD):** $\frac{1}{N} \sum |C_1 - C_2|$ (Pre-set Standard: $< 0.20$ students).
   - **Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC(2,1)):** Two-way random effects, absolute agreement (Pre-set Standard: $\ge 0.98$).
   - **Bland–Altman Plot & Limits of Agreement:** Mean difference (coder bias) and $95\%$ limits of agreement ($\text{Bias} \pm 1.96 \text{ SD}$).
   - *Pearson Correlation ($r$):* Reported as a secondary descriptive metric.
3. **Adjudication:** Any classroom observation with $|C_1 - C_2| \ge 1$ student must be visually re-examined by both coders jointly under high magnification to reach consensus.
4. **Internal Consistency Check:**
   $$\Delta \text{Enrollment} = \left| \sum \text{Homeroom Students} - \text{CCD Fall Membership} \right|$$
   If the yearbook count diverges from official October CCD membership by more than **$\pm 8\%$**, investigate the cause (e.g., missing Kindergarten pages, unphotographed mid-year transfer students).

## 7. Analytical Estimands & Hypothesis Tests

1. **Elementary Allocation Wedge:**
   $$\text{Elementary Wedge}_{s,t} = \text{Mean Homeroom Class Size}_{s,t} - \text{School Pupil/Teacher Ratio (CCD PTR)}_{s,t}$$
2. **Grade-Span Concordance:**
   - Test whether elementary homerooms (typically 20–24 students) exhibit an Allocation Wedge of **+4 to +7 students** above elementary building PTRs (typically 14–16:1), establishing that the wedge is a system-wide structural property of K–12 schooling.
3. **Decade Trend Test (H1b):**
   - Directly estimate within-school homeroom size changes over the 10-year period ($\Delta H = H_{2024-25} - H_{2014-15}$).
