# Research Questions: Kansas City Bi-State Education Capacity Study

## Primary Research Questions

1. **The Capacity Gap:**
   * What is the quantitative divergence between reported district pupil/teacher ratios and actual student classroom exposures in the Kansas City metropolitan area?
   * How does this divergence differ across school types (elementary vs. departmentalized middle/high school)?

2. **The "Donut" Geographic Distribution:**
   * How do school capacity indicators (class sizes, student/teacher ratios, support staff ratios) vary across the concentric rings of the Mid-America Regional Council (MARC) 9-county region?
   * When classified objectively by NCES standardized locale codes (City, Suburb, Town, Rural), do urban core, inner suburbs, outer high-growth suburbs, and rural districts demonstrate distinct capacity profiles?
   * Does distance from downtown Kansas City (continuous metric) correlate with classroom density or staffing pressure?

3. **Multi-Constraint Interactions (Classroom Load):**
   * Which schools and grade levels face compounding constraints (high student-teacher ratios combined with high IEP/ELL proportions, elevated chronic absenteeism, or high teacher turnover)?
   * Are high-complexity classrooms systematically paired with additional adult instructional capacity (e.g., paraprofessionals, co-teachers, interventionists), or do single teachers absorb the multi-constraint burden alone?

4. **Bi-State Policy Comparison:**
   * How do staffing capacity and classroom sizing compare across the Missouri–Kansas state line within a unified regional labor and housing market?
   * Do state-specific regulatory baselines (e.g., Missouri MSIP 6 class enrollment thresholds of 25 recommended / 33 maximum vs. Kansas open-enrollment capacity standards) produce measurable structural differences in classroom distributions?

5. **Counterfactual Scenarios & Feasibility:**
   * If core K–12 classes were capped at specific policy thresholds (e.g., 25 students, 22 students, or 17 for early elementary), how many additional full-time equivalent (FTE) classroom teachers and physical spaces would each district require?
   * What would the associated annual operating expenditure be under prevailing regional salary and benefit schedules?
