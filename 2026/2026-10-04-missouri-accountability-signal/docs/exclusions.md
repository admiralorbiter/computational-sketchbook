# Sample Exclusions and Analytic Samples

## 1. Analytic Sample Definitions

To prevent selection bias and maintain complete transparency, the master panel produces three nested analytic samples:

### Sample A — All Public Schools (Statewide Census)
- **Inclusion**: Every public school building reported in DESE MCDS files for a given year.
- **Purpose**: Statewide descriptive totals, universe accounting, and enrollment audits.

### Sample B — Conventional Accountability Schools (Primary Analytic Sample)
- **Inclusion**: Operational public schools serving tested grades with valid accountability outcomes.
- **Exclusions**:
  1. Early childhood centers without tested elementary grades.
  2. Pure Career and Technical Centers (CTCs / Area Tech Centers) without independent general accountability rosters.
  3. Specialized special education day schools and state schools for severely disabled students.
  4. Juvenile justice, correctional, and residential treatment center facilities.
  5. Schools with suppressed or zero accountable tested students.
- **Purpose**: Core predictive models, status vs. growth quadrant analysis, and APR decompositions.

### Sample C — Balanced Longitudinal Panel (2022–2025)
- **Inclusion**: Conventional accountability schools present with valid achievement, growth, and APR data across all four MSIP 6 years (2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025).
- **Purpose**: Longitudinal stability, transition matrices, and within-school change analysis.
