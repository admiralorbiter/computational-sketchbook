## 1. Concept & Hypothesis

Can navigating a Hamiltonian phase portrait $(x, p)$ become an intuitive and engaging physical game mechanic?

---

## 2. What Was Built

- **Phase-Space Hamiltonian Simulator:** Symplectic integration of position ($x$) and momentum ($p$) under conservative vector fields and potential wells.
- **Vector Field Visualization:** Streamline rendering and contour energy plotting in real-time.
- **Interactive Level Design:** Obstacles, energy barriers, and momentum-reversal gates.
